# Fidelity edge variants, version 1

The fidelity check (temporal-rift/simulation-workbench#60) plays the full case matrix for three variants: the baseline
and two variants at opposite edges of parameter space 1, so the simulator is compared with a real lane across the range
the configuration search can reach. Each edge variant is a set of overrides on the baseline, and both validate against
parameter space 1 (`docs/parameter-space/v1.md`).

- **`low`** sets as many parameters as the constraints allow at their range minimum.
- **`high`** sets as many parameters as the constraints allow at their range maximum.

Where constraints force a parameter away from the edge, the parameter earlier in the parameter-space order keeps the edge
and the later ones absorb the remainder. A value equal to the baseline is listed here and dropped when the experiment is
frozen, so the variant's served overrides omit it.

## Parameters off the edge

| Variant | Parameters | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `low` | `card-category-weights.DISRUPTION` 40, `card-category-weights.PARADOX` 30 | C-11: the four weights sum to 100 and their minimums sum to 45. At most two can hold their minimum (`PROBABILITY_SHIFTER` and `INFORMATION`, the first two in order); the other two take the only values that remain, their maximums |
| `low` | `card-grade-weights.I` 80 | C-12: the three weights sum to 100 and their minimums sum to 60. At most two can hold their minimum (`II` and `III`, the only pair that leaves `I` within its range); `I` takes the remainder, which is its maximum |
| `high` | `card-category-weights.INFORMATION` 10, `card-category-weights.DISRUPTION` 10 | C-11: the maximums sum to 160. At most two can hold their maximum (`PROBABILITY_SHIFTER` and `PARADOX`, the first pair in order that leaves the other two within range); the other two take their minimums, the only values that remain |
| `high` | `card-grade-weights.II` 15, `card-grade-weights.III` 5 | C-12: the maximums sum to 145. At most one can hold its maximum (`I`, the first in order); the others take their minimums, the only values that keep the sum at 100 with `I` at 80 |
| `high` | `victory.activist-consecutive-declarations` 3 | C-05: with `DECLARED_OUTCOME_WON_WITH_RALLY` at 8, a streak of 4 gives 4 × 8 = 32, not below the threshold 26. Lowering Rally instead would also lower `DECLARED_OUTCOME_WON` (C-13 needs Rally above it) and cost two parameters their maximum; lowering the streak costs one, and 3 × 8 = 24 < 26 |

Every other parameter holds the edge of its range, and every constraint of parameter space 1 holds for both sets.

## Validation

`FidelityEdgeVariantsTest` validates both sets through the workbench's variant rules against parameter space 1, and checks
that every key and value of `src/main/resources/fidelity/edge-variants-v1.json` appears in the table above and that the
table lists nothing else.
