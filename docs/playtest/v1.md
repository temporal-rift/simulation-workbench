# Playtest handoff version 1: negative result

Status: proposed for temporal-rift/simulation-workbench#62 (story S-15 of temporal-rift/infrastructure#124). No
campaign under the epic confirmed a candidate, so this is the negative-result summary, not a playtest brief. Design and
product approve it by approving its pull request.

**Bot results say nothing about balance among human players.** Everything below was played by the two bot policies,
`faction-greedy` 1.0.0 and `random` 1.0.0, on the simulator.

## Why nothing was short-listed

Every candidate of both decided campaigns fails gates **G-05** (each faction's win rate against an average seat, pooled,
band 0.5 to 2.0) and **G-06** (the same per faction set at 3 and 4 players). Some candidates of `bc3de6eb` also fail
G-04. The failure is one faction: **the Erasers win far more than their share**, and the other factions win too little.

Gate miss is each gate's mean deviation from its band, summed over the gates, so 0 means every gate met. The round-0

`GATES_NOT_MET` and `c-0000` (score 27.04) with `GATE_FAILS`, both failing G-05 and G-06. The best point of the 32 is
about 5% closer to the gates than the current rules, and the current rules reached the last round.

`c-0001` moves 31 parameters at once, including `game.rules.win-score-threshold` 23,
`game.rules.victory.weaver-chain-length` 4, `game.rules.cards-per-deal` 8 and scoring deltas such as
`CHAIN_COMPLETED` 11 and `MIMIC_NEVER_TRACED` 8; the full set is in the campaign log.

### The parameters do not reach the Eraser win share

After campaign `70c9e764`, one experiment set the Eraser and Prophet parameters of parameter space 1 at their range ends
(experiment `a72cfee3-50ce-4067-984d-c1224e3be534`, run `fc90e807-17d1-4211-bf68-30be57223b00`, `faction-greedy-v1`
table, seeds 8000001 and 8000002, 880 games, all succeeded). The Eraser G-05 ratio barely moved:

| Variant (overrides of `game.rules.`) | 3 players | 4 players | 5 players |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline | 2.69 | 3.48 | 5.00 |
| `victory.eraser-annihilations` 3 | 2.50 | 3.48 | 5.00 |
| `victory.eraser-annihilations` 5 | 2.50 | 3.48 | 5.00 |
| `scoring.score-deltas.ANNIHILATED_OUTCOME` 2 | 2.57 | 3.08 | 4.55 |
| Eraser nerf: `victory.eraser-annihilations` 5, `scoring.score-deltas.ANNIHILATED_OUTCOME` 2 | 2.31 | 3.52 | 5.00 |
| `win-score-threshold` 26 | 2.54 | 3.40 | 5.00 |
| Prophet boost: `victory.prophet-written-resolutions` 3, `stabilization.prophet-written-resolutions` 2 | 2.07 | 3.27 | 5.00 |
| Eraser nerf and Prophet boost | 2.40 | 3.41 | 4.50 |

The overrides do apply: the Erasers' winning score follows the annihilation objective (9 at 3 annihilations, 12 at 4,
15 at 5). Their win share does not change, so they win by a path these parameters do not touch. The Prophet boost
raises the Prophets from 0.22 to 0.80 at 3 and 4 players, which shows the parameters can move a faction; they cannot
move this one.

## What the evidence suggests to change next

- **The parameter space, not the budget.** A larger search of parameter space 1 would not help: its parameters at their
  range ends leave the Erasers above the band, and the version 2 search kept the baseline among its best points to the
  end. The next search needs rule levers that act on how the Erasers win. The Eraser balance work is tracked in
  temporal-rift/game-service#350; on/off rule parameters for the workbench are temporal-rift/simulation-workbench#148.
  A new parameter space version built on those rules is the input for the next campaign.
- **The criterion stays.** G-05 and G-06 fail by wide margins with tight intervals, not near a band edge, so neither
  the bands nor the sample size decide this result.
- **Check the bot before the rules.** Both gates are measured on the `faction-greedy` table. Part of the Eraser margin
  may come from how that policy plays the Erasers rather than from the rules. A rules change that closes the gap for
  `faction-greedy` should still be checked against another policy before a playtest.

## What this evidence cannot show

- Anything about human play: the bots follow fixed policies, and the gates are measured on their games only.
- Anything about other bot policies than `faction-greedy` 1.0.0 and `random` 1.0.0.
- Anything about rules outside parameter space 1, including the Eraser rules a fix would change.

**Bot results say nothing about balance among human players.**
