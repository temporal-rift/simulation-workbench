# Budget record version 1

Status: proposed for the configuration search (temporal-rift/infrastructure#124, decision P-01, computed in
temporal-rift/simulation-workbench#58). It becomes approved when design and product approve the pull request that adds
it; any amendment is made in that review or in a later version, never by editing an approved version.

The record fixes the search parameters that fit the default budget on the simulator and the time the confirmation
needs on a light lane, both computed from measured capacity. The search parameters are the default `SearchAlgorithm`
values of a new campaign (`search-budgets/v1.json`, equal to the table under "Search parameters").

**Result.** The search parameters fit 4 active hours only as a screen of four candidates on two seeds each, which
cannot reach an evidence-grade verdict on any scored line. The seeds one candidate needs to be told `MEETS` from
`FAILS` cost five times the whole budget. The confirmation takes 250 active hours on one light lane. Neither part
meets its purpose on the measured capacity; "Fit" names the gaps, and the decision returns to design (the
confirmation sample) and product (the time).

## Inputs

| Input | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Reference spec | 4 cores / 8 threads, 16 GB | Both capacity reports |
| Simulator games per hour, `Rs` | 407 | [docs/capacity/simulator-v1.md](../capacity/simulator-v1.md), 4 slots |
| Light-lane games per hour, `Rl` | 88 | [docs/capacity/per-game-context.md](../capacity/per-game-context.md), repetition 2 |
| Active hours of the budget, `H` | 4 | Default budget of a campaign |
| Balance criterion | version 1 | [docs/balance-criteria/v1.md](../balance-criteria/v1.md) |
| Parameter space | version 1 | [docs/parameter-space/v1.md](../parameter-space/v1.md) |

- **`Rs`.** The simulator report measures 104, 212, 407, 422, 433, 432 and 424 games per hour at 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16 and
  20 slots. From 4 slots on the rate is flat (407 to 433) and only memory grows, so the record takes the 4-slot
  figure: the lowest of the plateau and the one with the least memory (5,888 MiB peak heap). The workload of that
  measurement is three-player cases only, the smallest shape; four- and five-player games take longer, so the real
  rate over the full matrix is lower.
- **`Rl`.** The light-lane report measures 89 and 88 games per hour in its two repetitions. The record takes the lower.
  The figure counts only the 192 of 220 games that succeeded, and the failures removed mostly four- and five-player
  games, so the rate over the full matrix is lower.

## The matrix

One seed plays every case of the full matrix (criterion D-09): every faction set at 3, 4 and 5 players, every cyclic
rotation, both policies. A faction set of `p` players has `p` rotations, and the five factions give `C(5, p)` sets:

| Players `p` | Faction sets `C(5, p)` | Rotations | Games per seed and policy | Seat-games of one faction per seed and policy |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 10 | 3 | 30 | 18 (in 6 sets × 3) |
| 4 | 5 | 4 | 20 | 16 (in 4 sets × 4) |
| 5 | 1 | 5 | 5 | 5 |

Games per seed, `g = 2 × (30 + 20 + 5) = 110`. The criterion's 100 confirmation seeds are 100 × 110 = 11,000 games,
the figure the issue calls "about 11,000".

## Seeds per candidate

A candidate is told `MEETS` from `FAILS` on a line when the half-width of the 95% interval is smaller than the
distance from the line's value to the band edge. For a mean of per-seed values with variance `σ²` over `n` seeds the
half-width is `z σ / √n`, with `z = 1.96`, so

```
n ≥ z² σ² / h²        with h the distance to the edge
```

and for a rate over `m` seat-games per seed, `σ² = π (1 − π) / m` with `π` the rate. Seat-games of one seed are
treated as independent and `R` as known, as criterion D-09 does; they are positively correlated in fact, which only
raises `n`.

### L-01, the narrowest scored band, `[0.8, 1.25]`

A faction at parity has ratio 1, so the nearer edge is 0.8 and the margin is 0.2 of `R`. In rate terms `h = 0.2 × π`
with `π = R = 1/p`:

| `p` | `π` | `m` | `σ² = π (1 − π) / m` | `h = 0.2 π` | `n = 3.8416 σ² / h²` | Seeds |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 0.3333 | 18 | 0.012346 | 0.06667 | 10.67 | 11 |
| 4 | 0.2500 | 16 | 0.011719 | 0.05000 | 18.01 | 19 |
| 5 | 0.2000 | 5 | 0.032000 | 0.04000 | 76.83 | 77 |

Five players bind: 77 seeds. The upper edge (1.25, a margin of 0.25) needs fewer.

### G-01, `failure_share ≤ 0.02`

At five players a policy plays 5 games per seed (`m = 5`), the binding case again. A candidate with a true share of
0.01 shows `MEETS` when `h = 0.01`; one with a true share of 0.04 shows `FAILS` when `h = 0.02`:

| True share `π` | `σ² = π (1 − π) / 5` | `h` | `n = 3.8416 σ² / h²` | Seeds |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.01 | 0.001980 | 0.01 | 76.06 | 77 |
| 0.04 | 0.007680 | 0.02 | 73.76 | 74 |

If no game fails at all, the least evidence for `≤ 0.02` is the smallest `N` with `0.98^N ≤ 0.05`:
`N ≥ ln 0.05 / ln 0.98 = 148.3`, so 149 games, which is 30 seeds at five players.

### Means (L-02, `eras` mean in `[3.5, 4.5]`)

`eras` lies in 1 to 5, so a game's standard deviation is at most 2. At parity (mean 4) the margin is 0.5; at five
players with `m = 5` games per seed, `σ² ≤ 4 / 5 = 0.8` and `n ≥ 3.8416 × 0.8 / 0.25 = 12.29`, so 13 seeds. Means do not
bind.

### Result

**77 seeds per candidate**, from L-01 and G-01 at five players. The criterion's confirmation minimum of 100 seeds is
that figure plus 30% for the correlation of seat-games within a seed.

One candidate at 77 seeds is 77 × 110 = 8,470 games: `8,470 / 407 = 20.8` h on the simulator and `8,470 / 88 = 96.3` h
on a light lane.

## Search parameters

The search is successive halving (`SUCCESSIVE_HALVING_V1`). Round `r` has `nᵣ = ⌈N / etaʳ⌉` candidates, each
evaluated on the first `sᵣ = screeningSeeds × etaʳ` seeds, and ends when at most `shortListSize` candidates are left.
Planning assumes that no gate discards a candidate, the most expensive case, and that the seeds of a round extend those
of the round before, so a survivor plays only the seeds it has not played. The budget is `H × Rs = 4 × 407 = 1,628`
games, which is `1,628 / 110 = 14.8` seeds of the full matrix, summed over candidates.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| `initialCandidates` `N` | 4 |
| `screeningSeeds` | 2 |
| `eta` | 2 |
| `shortListSize` | 1 |

`screeningSeeds` is 2 because an evaluation on fewer than two seeds cannot reject anything (criterion D-09), `eta` is
2, its minimum, to keep the most candidates, and `shortListSize` is 1 because two halvings of 4 candidates leave one
and the confirmation cost grows with it. With one more candidate or one more seed the plan no longer fits:

| Round | Candidates `nᵣ` | Seeds `sᵣ` | New seeds per candidate | Games `nᵣ × new × 110` | Cumulative games | Hours at 407/h |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 880 | 880 | 2.16 |
| 1 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 440 | 1,320 | 3.24 |

Total: 1,320 games, 3.24 h, within the 1,628 games and 4 h; 308 games (0.76 h) stay unspent. Without reuse of played
games the total is 880 + 2 × 4 × 110 = 1,760 games (4.32 h), which does not fit, so reuse is part of this record.

Alternatives at the same budget and `shortListSize` 1: `N = 5` needs three rounds, 24 seeds of the matrix or 2,640
games (6.49 h), and does not fit; `screeningSeeds` 3 with `N = 4` needs 1,980 games (4.87 h) and does not fit; `N = 3`
fits in 1,100 games (2.70 h) with fewer candidates; a single round of `N = 7` with `eta = 7` fits in 1,540 games
(3.78 h) and picks one candidate out of seven on two seeds. `N = 4` is the largest halving plan that fits.

## Confirmation duration

```
games per run = 100 seeds × 110 = 11,000
duration = games per run × (shortListSize + 1) / Rl = 11,000 × 2 / 88 = 250 h
```

One run per short-listed candidate and one for the baseline, played one after another on one light lane: 250 active
hours, 10.4 days. This is a lower bound, because `Rl` is the rate of the games that succeeded.

## Fit

| Part | Needs | Has | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search parameters | 1,320 games | 1,628 games (4 h) | Yes, by construction |
| Seeds to tell `MEETS` from `FAILS` for one candidate | 77 seeds, 8,470 games, 20.8 h | 14.8 seeds, 1,628 games, 4 h | **No**: 19% of one candidate |
| Confirmation | 250 h | the time product accepts | **No** against 4 h or 24 h |

- **The search.** The default plan fits because it is a screen: the baseline and three perturbations on two seeds each.
  The half-width of an L-01 interval at five players is `1.96 × 0.179 / √s` in rate (0.248 at two seeds and 0.175 at
  four), against a band half-width of 0.04, so no scored line can be `MEETS` or `FAILS`. Only a gate with a limit of 0
  that fails in every game (G-02, G-03) can discard a candidate on evidence, and the plan covers 4 of the 90 or so
  one-step perturbations. A search worth running needs a multiple of the budget: each candidate screened at the 77
  seeds costs 20.8 h on the simulator.
- **The confirmation.** At 100 seeds it is 250 h. The gap to 24 h is 226 h, or 11 lanes if throughput scaled linearly
  with lanes (not measured). The duration is `2.5 × seeds` h for a short list of one:

  | Confirmation seeds | Hours |
  |---:|---:|
  | 100 | 250 |
  | 77 | 192.5 |
  | 30 | 75 |
  | 9 | 22.5 |

  A confirmation inside 24 h on one lane allows 9 seeds, which is below the 77 that decide a faction line.

The levers are the confirmation sample (design: the criterion's 100 seeds, or the matrix per seed), the time (product),
the number of lanes and the simulator's rate (more threads than the reference spec, since the rate is CPU-bound). Until
design and product choose, the search stays a screen and the confirmation stays out of reach on one lane.
