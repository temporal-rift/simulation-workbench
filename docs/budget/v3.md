# Budget record version 3

Status: proposed for the configuration search (temporal-rift/infrastructure#124, decision P-01), computed in
temporal-rift/simulation-workbench#136. It supersedes version 2 ([v2.md](v2.md)), which is unchanged, when design and
product approve the pull request that adds it; any amendment is made in that review or in a later version, never by
editing an approved version.

Version 2 priced the confirmation on a light lane, as a baseline run plus one run per short-listed candidate, and rested
on two unmeasured inputs. Since then the confirmation moves to the simulator and plays no baseline
(temporal-rift/simulation-workbench#135), and balance criterion version 2 adds a confirmation-only table
(temporal-rift/simulation-workbench#134). This record replaces the confirmation duration and measures the two inputs.

**Result.** The search parameters are version 2's: a halving plan of 32 candidates on two seeds each, narrowed to one,
21,120 games, 3.91 h at the simulator rate. The confirmation is 104 seeds × 165 games = 17,160 games per candidate:
**3.18 h on the simulator** at that rate, with no baseline run and no light lane. The confirmation matrix was played on
the baseline rules on the simulator, as a confirmation plays it
([docs/capacity/confirmation-matrix-v1.md](../capacity/confirmation-matrix-v1.md)): 17,160 games, all of them with a
result, at 5,579 games an hour over its last 2.4 hours. The within-seed correlation of `faction-greedy-v1` is
0.010, −0.009 and 0.001 at 3, 4 and 5 players, far below the 0.079 that sized the sample, so **no player count needs
more than 104 seeds** (the largest need is 91, at the upper end of the five-player interval). The baseline rules are
`REJECTED` in confirmation mode.

There is no light-lane input: version 2's `Rl` priced a confirmation that no longer exists. `Rs` is version 2's figure,
the rate over the full 110-case matrix at 12 slots. The measurement of the confirmation matrix, which is 165 cases per
seed, played at 5,579 games an hour over its last 2.4 hours (see "Confirmation duration"), so `Rs` stays the conservative
planning figure.

## The matrix

One seed plays every case of the full matrix: every faction set at 3, 4 and 5 players and every cyclic rotation. A
faction set of `p` players has `p` rotations, and the five factions give `C(5, p)` sets, so a table plays
`10 × 3 + 5 × 4 + 1 × 5 = 55` games per seed.

| Phase | Tables | Games per seed `g` |
|---|---|---:|
| Screening (the search) | `faction-greedy-v1`, `random-v1` | 2 × 55 = 110 |
| Confirmation | the same two and `random-seat-vs-faction-greedy-v1` | 3 × 55 = 165 |

## Search parameters

The screening tables of criterion version 2 play the same 110 games per seed as version 1, so version 2's computation
carries over unchanged and is restated in full. The search is successive halving (`SUCCESSIVE_HALVING_V1`). Round `r`
has `nᵣ = ⌈N / etaʳ⌉` candidates, each evaluated on the first `sᵣ = screeningSeeds × etaʳ` seeds, and ends when at most
`shortListSize` candidates are left. Planning assumes that no gate discards a candidate, the most expensive case, and
that the seeds of a round extend those of the round before, so a survivor plays only the seeds it has not played. The
budget is `H × Rs = 4 × 5,402 = 21,608` games, which is `21,608 / 110 = 196.4` seeds of the screening matrix, summed over
candidates.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| `initialCandidates` `N` | 32 |
| `screeningSeeds` | 2 |
| `eta` | 2 |
| `shortListSize` | 1 |

| Round | Candidates `nᵣ` | Seeds `sᵣ` | New seeds per candidate | Games `nᵣ × new × 110` | Cumulative games | Hours at 5,402/h |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 32 | 2 | 2 | 7,040 | 7,040 | 1.30 |
| 1 | 16 | 4 | 2 | 3,520 | 10,560 | 1.95 |
| 2 | 8 | 8 | 4 | 3,520 | 14,080 | 2.61 |
| 3 | 4 | 16 | 8 | 3,520 | 17,600 | 3.26 |
| 4 | 2 | 32 | 16 | 3,520 | 21,120 | 3.91 |

Total: 21,120 games, 3.91 h, within the 21,608 games and 4 h; 488 games (0.09 h) stay unspent. The plan needs
`21,120 / 4 = 5,280` games per hour, so it holds while the real rate stays above 5,280, **2.3% below `Rs`**. Without
reuse of played games the total is 320 seeds, 35,200 games (6.52 h), which does not fit. `N = 33` needs 288 seeds,
31,680 games (5.86 h), so 32 is the largest halving plan at `screeningSeeds` 2. The reasons for each value, and the
limits of a plan this size, are those of version 2: it is a screen, and it covers about two candidates decisively.

## Confirmation duration

```
games per candidate = confirmationMinimumSeeds × games per seed of the CONFIRMATION tables = 104 × 165 = 17,160
duration = games per candidate × shortListSize / Rs = 17,160 / 5,402 = 3.18 h
```

There is no baseline run and no light lane. The confirmation plays one run per short-listed candidate on the simulator,
one after another.

**Measured.** The confirmation matrix of criterion version 2 was played on the baseline rules on the simulator, seeds
5001 to 5104, as a confirmation plays it, and every one of its 17,160 games has a result
([docs/capacity/confirmation-matrix-v1.md](../capacity/confirmation-matrix-v1.md)). The run's wall time was 4.33 h,
3,960 games an hour, but it was not played at a steady rate: it was interrupted and resumed, and its first 1.8 hours shared
the machine with other work and a database that fsynced every write. After the database was relieved of that, the last
13,177 games took 2.36 h, **5,579 games an hour, 3.08 h for 17,160 games**, and the last 1.7 h played 6,243 an hour
(2.75 h). Both are above `Rs`, so 3.18 h is a conservative duration for the matrix on these builds and this machine; the
measured wall time of 4.33 h is the duration of that run as it happened, with its interruptions. The builds and the
fidelity record the simulator embeds are in the capacity record.

## Seeds the confirmation needs

The sample is criterion version 2's **104 seeds**. Version 2 of this record sized it at five players from the
within-seed correlation of `faction-greedy-v1`, 0.079 at five players on 800 games, which scaled the 79 independent
seeds of G-05 by `1 + 4ρ`. Measured on the confirmation matrix, with the same estimator and a 95% bootstrap interval over
the 104 seeds:

| Players | Seat-games per seed `m` | `ρ` (95% interval) | Independent seeds `n⁰` | Seeds `⌈n⁰ (1 + (m − 1) ρ)⌉` at `ρ` (interval) |
|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| 3 | 18 | 0.010 ([−0.005, 0.025]) | 12 | 15 (12 to 18) |
| 4 | 16 | −0.009 ([−0.022, 0.004]) | 19 | 19 (19 to 21) |
| 5 | 5 | 0.001 ([−0.013, 0.035]) | 79 | 80 (79 to 91) |

`n⁰` is G-05 at ratio 0.8 with 95% confidence and 90% power, as criterion version 2 states it. **No player count
needs more than 104 seeds**, and the highest need at the upper end of any interval is 91, so the rule that raises the
sample does not fire and the sample stays 104. The measured `ρ` would size the sample at 80, but this record does not
change it: that needs criterion version 3. The correlation under `random-v1` is −0.001 at 3 and 4 players and −0.013 at
five players, all intervals containing 0. The five-player figure of version 2 (0.079, [0.001, 0.175], on 80 seeds) is
not reproduced: the new interval, on 104 seeds and 520 five-player games, overlaps the old one only at its low end.

## Baseline faction ratios

Each faction's win rate over `R`, on the criterion's definition (every winner scores a whole win, `R` is the mean winners
per game over `p`). The full tables with intervals are in the capacity record. On `faction-greedy-v1` the ratios are far
outside G-05's band `[0.5, 2.0]`: ERASERS 2.22, 3.04 and 4.89 at 3, 4 and 5 players, and PROPHETS 0.29, 0.18 and 0.01. On
the random seat of the mixed table (G-07, limit 1.0), **every faction at every player count is `MEETS`**: the highest
value is ACTIVISTS at 0.34 at five players (interval [0.15, 0.56]). Version 2 of this record flagged G-07 as
incompatible with the baseline's random-play ratio of 2.20; on the mixed table that conflict is gone.

The baseline's verdict in confirmation mode under criterion version 2 is **`REJECTED`**: 80 lines `MEETS`, 65
`FAILS` (G-05 10, G-06 35, L-01 13, L-05 1, L-06 3, L-07 3) and 4 `INCONCLUSIVE` (G-01 at five players and 3 lines of
G-06). That is the baseline's state, not a property of the budget: the search exists to move candidates off it.

## Open questions

- The time product accepts for the confirmation: 3.18 h on the simulator, down from 100 h on a light lane.
- Whether the plan keeps the largest halving (32 candidates, a 2.3% margin) or a smaller one with room (16 candidates,
  1.63 h), as in version 2.
- Whether criterion version 3 lowers the sample to match the measured correlation (80 seeds at the estimate, 91 at the
  upper end of its interval). A shorter confirmation then costs 17,160 × 80 / 104 games.
- `Rs` on a machine with no other load: the measurement here ran with other work on the machine for its first hours.
- A re-attempt of a simulator case after an interruption reuses the command ledger of the earlier attempt but plays a
  new game, and never reaches an ending. The measurement lost 10 cases to it (see the capacity record).
