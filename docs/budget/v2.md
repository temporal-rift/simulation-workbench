# Budget record version 2

Status: proposed for the configuration search (temporal-rift/infrastructure#124, decision P-01), computed in
temporal-rift/simulation-workbench#110 from the capacity measured after #103. It supersedes version 1
([v1.md](v1.md)), which is unchanged, when design and product approve the pull request that adds it; any amendment is
made in that review or in a later version, never by editing an approved version.

The record fixes the search parameters that fit the default budget on the simulator and the time the confirmation
needs on a light lane, both computed from measured capacity. The search parameters are the default `SearchAlgorithm`
values of a new campaign (`search-budgets/v2.json`, equal to the table under "Search parameters").

**Result.** The search parameters fit 4 active hours as a halving plan of 32 candidates on two seeds each, narrowed to
one, 192 seeds of the matrix in all (3.91 h). That is a screen: its first round plays two seeds per candidate, which
cannot reach an evidence-grade verdict on any scored line, and a decisive verdict on L-01 for one candidate takes 77
seeds at the least (1.57 h), so the budget covers about two candidates decisively. The precision a search needs to rank
candidates is not calculated. The confirmation takes 96 active hours on one light lane. Design's answer, a confirmation
sample of 100 seeds, holds on the measured within-seed correlation (0.037), but the highest random-play faction ratio is
2.20, above the 1.6 it assumed; the time returns to product.

| Input | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Reference spec | 4 cores / 8 threads, 16 GB | Both capacity reports |
| Simulator games per hour, `Rs` | 5,402 | [docs/capacity/simulator-v2.md](../capacity/simulator-v2.md), 12 slots |
| Light-lane games per hour, `Rl` | 229 | [docs/capacity/per-game-context-v2.md](../capacity/per-game-context-v2.md), repetition 1 |

- **`Rs`.** The simulator report measures 1,424, 2,227, 4,003, 6,309 and 5,402 games per hour at 1, 2, 4, 8 and 12
  slots over the full 110-case matrix. The rate peaks at 8 slots and falls 14% at 12, so the record takes the 12-slot
  figure: the lowest of the 8 to 12-slot plateau, and the only one measured on the service builds that include the fix
  for stalled games (temporal-rift/game-service#345), so it carries no qualification that the run happened not to
  stall. Its peak heap is 7,681 MiB. Two cautions: the 12-slot run shared the machine with a browser and the desktop
  client, so it may understate the rate, and the 8-slot figure, 17% higher, was measured on the earlier builds. The
  workload is the full matrix, so the rate already includes the longer four- and five-player games.
- **`Rl`.** The light-lane report measures 229 and 243 games per hour in its two repetitions, after games settled on
  consumer offsets (#103) removed the 1 s floor under each decision point. The record takes the lower. Every game of
  both repetitions succeeded, so the figure is the rate over the full matrix.

## The matrix

One seed plays every case of the full matrix (criterion D-09): every faction set at 3, 4 and 5 players, every cyclic
rotation, both policies. A faction set of `p` players has `p` rotations, and the five factions give `C(5, p)` sets:

| Players `p` | Faction sets `C(5, p)` | Rotations | Games per seed and policy | Seat-games of one faction per seed and policy |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 10 | 3 | 30 | 18 (in 6 sets × 3) |
| 4 | 5 | 4 | 20 | 16 (in 4 sets × 4) |
| 5 | 1 | 5 | 5 | 5 |

Games per seed, `g = 2 × (30 + 20 + 5) = 110`. The criterion's 100 confirmation seeds are 100 × 110 = 11,000 games,
the figure the issue calls "about 11,000".

## Seeds per candidate

A candidate is told `MEETS` from `FAILS` on a line when the half-width of the 95% interval is smaller than the
distance from the line's value to the band edge. For a mean of per-seed values with variance `σ²` over `n` seeds the
half-width is `z σ / √n`, with `z = 1.96`, so

```
n ≥ z² σ² / h²        with h the distance to the edge
```

and for a rate over `m` seat-games per seed, `σ² = π (1 − π) / m` with `π` the rate. Seat-games of one seed are
treated as independent and `R` as known, as criterion D-09 does; they are positively correlated in fact, which only
raises `n`.

### L-01, the narrowest scored band, `[0.8, 1.25]`

A faction at parity has ratio 1, so the nearer edge is 0.8 and the margin is 0.2 of `R`. In rate terms `h = 0.2 × π`
with `π = R = 1/p`:

| `p` | `π` | `m` | `σ² = π (1 − π) / m` | `h = 0.2 π` | `n = 3.8416 σ² / h²` | Seeds |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 0.3333 | 18 | 0.012346 | 0.06667 | 10.67 | 11 |
| 4 | 0.2500 | 16 | 0.011719 | 0.05000 | 18.01 | 19 |
| 5 | 0.2000 | 5 | 0.032000 | 0.04000 | 76.83 | 77 |

Five players bind: 77 seeds. The upper edge (1.25, a margin of 0.25) needs fewer.

### G-01, `failure_share ≤ 0.02`

At five players a policy plays 5 games per seed (`m = 5`), the binding case again. A candidate with a true share of
0.01 shows `MEETS` when `h = 0.01`; one with a true share of 0.04 shows `FAILS` when `h = 0.02`:

| True share `π` | `σ² = π (1 − π) / 5` | `h` | `n = 3.8416 σ² / h²` | Seeds |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.01 | 0.001980 | 0.01 | 76.06 | 77 |
| 0.04 | 0.007680 | 0.02 | 73.76 | 74 |

If no game fails at all, the least evidence for `≤ 0.02` is the smallest `N` with `0.98^N ≤ 0.05`:
`N ≥ ln 0.05 / ln 0.98 = 148.3`, so 149 games, which is 30 seeds at five players.

### Means (L-02, `eras` mean in `[3.5, 4.5]`)

`eras` lies in 1 to 5, so a game's standard deviation is at most 2. At parity (mean 4) the margin is 0.5; at five
players with `m = 5` games per seed, `σ² ≤ 4 / 5 = 0.8` and `n ≥ 3.8416 × 0.8 / 0.25 = 12.29`, so 13 seeds. Means do not
bind.

### Result

**77 seeds per candidate**, from L-01 and G-01 at five players. Both lines are evaluated per policy (L-01 on
`faction-greedy-v1`, G-01 per policy and player count), so `m = 5` seat-games per seed holds; it would be 10 only for a
line that pools both policies; design confirms that none does.

**What 77 means.** The formula sets the expected half-width of the interval equal to the margin. A faction exactly at
parity then clears its nearer edge in about half of the samples, so 77 seeds is the precision at which a verdict
becomes possible, not a decision rule. For 80% power the factor is `(1.96 + 0.84)² / 1.96² = 2.04`:

| Line | Seeds for half-width = margin | Seeds for 80% power |
|---|---:|---:|
| L-01 at five players | 77 | 157 |
| G-01 at five players, true share 0.01 | 77 | 156 |
| G-01 at five players, true share 0.04 | 74 | 151 |

Three limits apply to these figures:

- **Criterion text.** Criterion D-09 derives "about 70" seeds from a distance of "0.2 R, about 0.044" and sets the
  minimum at 100, because seat-games of one seed are correlated and `R` is estimated. `0.2 × 0.2` is 0.04, which gives
  the 77 above; 100 is the criterion's own rounding, not 77 plus a stated margin. D-12 repeats the 0.044. Correcting
  the text needs a later criterion version.
- **Interval.** The criterion uses a percentile block-bootstrap interval; this record uses normal theory, as the issue
  asks. They differ for rare failures: with no failed game the bootstrap interval is `[0, 0]`, so G-01 would be `MEETS`
  on two seeds, and the 149-game bound above is a binomial bound the criterion's interval does not apply. With about
  four failures in 385 games the normal approximation is not reliable either.
- **What each phase needs.** Confirmation needs every pooled gate to `MEET` and no line to `FAIL`; an `INCONCLUSIVE`
  scored line such as L-01 is allowed, so the pooled gates set the confirmation sample (see "Seeds the confirmation
  needs"). The search ranks by point-value score and discards only on a `FAILS` gate, so it needs enough precision to
  rank candidates, which is a different calculation from this one and is not made here.

One candidate at 77 seeds is 77 × 110 = 8,470 games: `8,470 / 5,402 = 1.57` h on the simulator and `8,470 / 229 = 37.0` h
on a light lane. At 157 seeds it is 17,270 games: 3.20 h and 75.4 h.

## Seeds the confirmation needs

Design answer. Confirmation needs every pooled gate to `MEET`. The candidate it confirms is the search's best, whose
point values lie inside the scored bands, so each gate is sized for the least favourable value that still meets the
scored targets, at five players (`m = 5` per seed and policy), with

```
n ≥ (z + z_β)² σ² / h²        z = 1.96, z_β = 0.84 for 80% and 1.28 for 90% power
```

| Gate | Least favourable value on target | `h` | Seeds at 80% | Seeds at 90% |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| G-05, `faction-greedy-v1` ratio in `[0.5, 2.0]` | ratio 0.8, the lower edge of L-01 (`π = 0.16`) | 0.06 | 59 | 79 |
| G-07, `random-v1` ratio `≤ 2.0` | ratio 1.6 (`π = 0.32`) | 0.08 | 54 | 72 |
| G-04, share of games ending by era 2 `< 0.1` | 0.03, or 0.05 | 0.07, or 0.05 | 10, or 30 | 13, or 40 |
| G-08, `shared_win_rate ≤ 0.5` | 0.2, the limit of L-05 | 0.3 | 3 | 4 |
| G-01, G-02, G-03 | 0 | | 2 | 2 |

G-04 is evaluated as the share of games that end by era 2, since its quantile 0.1 is at least 3 exactly when that
share is below 0.1. At three and four players G-05 needs 9 and 14 seeds at 80% (12 and 19 at 90%), so five players
binds.

- **Power per line.** There are 57 pooled gate lines (30 for `faction-greedy-v1`, 27 for `random-v1`), but a candidate
  on target is near an edge in about two: the weakest greedy faction on G-05 and the strongest random faction on G-07,
  both at five players. 90% per line keeps the chance that both `MEET` at about 80%.
- **Correlation.** Seat-games of one seed share their deck and deals. With a within-seed correlation `ρ` the seeds
  scale by `1 + (m − 1) ρ = 1 + 4ρ` at five players: the 79 seeds of G-05 become 100 at `ρ = 0.066` and 111 at
  `ρ = 0.1`.
- **G-01, G-02 and G-03 are sized for zero.** The design target for stalled games and abnormal endings is zero, not
  the limit. With no occurrence the bootstrap interval is `[0, 0]` and the line `MEETS` on any sample. A candidate with
  recurring counting failures is not confirmed by playing more seeds (a true G-01 share of 0.01 needs 156 seeds at
  80%): it ends `UNCONFIRMED` and the failures are investigated as defects. With none observed, 100 seeds bound the
  five-player rate below `3 / 500 = 0.6%`.
- **G-07 beyond 1.6.** A random-play faction at ratio 1.7 needs 131 seeds at 90%, and one at 1.8 needs 303. Such a
  faction is close to the defect the gate catches, so its candidate ending `UNCONFIRMED` is accepted instead of a
  larger sample.

**Answer: the confirmation sample stays at 100 seeds.** It is about 80 seeds if the seat-games of one seed were
independent and 100 at `ρ ≈ 0.07`, bound by G-05 and G-07 at five players, not by L-01. The figure holds while two
measurements stay within these assumptions, and both were measured on 800 five-player games
([docs/capacity/five-player-analysis-v2.md](../capacity/five-player-analysis-v2.md)):

1. the within-seed correlation of seat-games at five players, `ρ ≤ 0.07`. **Measured 0.037** (standard error 0.023,
   95% interval [−0.006, 0.077]): the assumption holds at the estimate, but the interval's upper end is above 0.07
   and would raise the sample to 104 seeds (the 79-seed term exceeds 100 above `ρ = 0.067`). The correlation is in
   `faction-greedy-v1` (0.079); under `random-v1` it is −0.005.
2. the highest faction ratio under `random-v1` at five players, `≤ 1.6`. **Measured 2.20** (REVISIONISTS, interval
   [1.97, 2.44]); ACTIVISTS is at 1.82. The assumption does not hold. The sample stays at 100 seeds, as the rule says,
   and a candidate with a random-play faction above 1.6 may end `UNCONFIRMED`. The baseline rules are themselves at
   2.20, and G-07's limit of 2.0 lies at the lower end of REVISIONISTS' interval, so a candidate near the baseline is
   likely to end `UNCONFIRMED` on G-07. That is a question for the criterion, not for the sample size.

If `ρ` is higher than measured, the sample is raised by the factor `1 + 4ρ` (criterion D-09 sets 100 as a minimum). The
figures use normal theory; the criterion's bootstrap interval is expected to behave alike at these rates, which is not
checked here.

## Search parameters

The search is successive halving (`SUCCESSIVE_HALVING_V1`). Round `r` has `nᵣ = ⌈N / etaʳ⌉` candidates, each
evaluated on the first `sᵣ = screeningSeeds × etaʳ` seeds, and ends when at most `shortListSize` candidates are left.
Planning assumes that no gate discards a candidate, the most expensive case, and that the seeds of a round extend those
of the round before, so a survivor plays only the seeds it has not played. The budget is `H × Rs = 4 × 5,402 = 21,608`
games, which is `21,608 / 110 = 196.4` seeds of the full matrix, summed over candidates.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| `initialCandidates` `N` | 32 |
| `screeningSeeds` | 2 |
| `eta` | 2 |
| `shortListSize` | 1 |

`screeningSeeds` is 2 because an evaluation on fewer than two seeds cannot reject anything (criterion D-09), `eta` is
2, its minimum, to keep the most candidates, and `shortListSize` is 1 because the halvings of 32 candidates leave one
and the confirmation cost grows with it. With one more candidate the plan no longer fits:

| Round | Candidates `nᵣ` | Seeds `sᵣ` | New seeds per candidate | Games `nᵣ × new × 110` | Cumulative games | Hours at 5,402/h |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 32 | 2 | 2 | 7,040 | 7,040 | 1.30 |
| 1 | 16 | 4 | 2 | 3,520 | 10,560 | 1.95 |
| 2 | 8 | 8 | 4 | 3,520 | 14,080 | 2.61 |
| 3 | 4 | 16 | 8 | 3,520 | 17,600 | 3.26 |
| 4 | 2 | 32 | 16 | 3,520 | 21,120 | 3.91 |

Total: 21,120 games, 3.91 h, within the 21,608 games and 4 h; 488 games (0.09 h) stay unspent. The plan needs
`21,120 / 4 = 5,280` games per hour, so it holds while the real rate stays above 5,280, **2.3% below `Rs`**. That margin
is thin: a slower machine, or the heavier load the 12-slot measurement ran under, ends the plan early. Without reuse of
played games the total is 320 seeds, 35,200 games (6.52 h), which does not fit, so reuse is part of this record.

Alternatives at the same budget: `N = 33` needs 288 seeds, 31,680 games (5.86 h), and does not fit, so 32 is the
largest halving plan at `screeningSeeds` 2; `screeningSeeds` 3 fits `N = 18` in 195 seeds, 21,450 games (3.97 h), with a
margin of 0.7%; `N = 16` at `screeningSeeds` 2 needs 80 seeds, 8,800 games (1.63 h), and holds while the rate stays above
2,200 games an hour, 59% below `Rs`. Choosing the largest plan follows version 1's rule; the smaller plan is the one with a
real margin, and design and product may prefer it.

## Confirmation duration

```
games per run = 100 seeds × 110 = 11,000
duration = games per run × (shortListSize + 1) / Rl = 11,000 × 2 / 229 = 96.1 h
```

One run per short-listed candidate and one for the baseline, played one after another on one light lane: 96 active
hours, 4.0 days. At the upper end of the `ρ` interval (104 seeds) it is 99.9 h. `Rl` is the rate over the full matrix
with no failed game, so unlike version 1 the figure is not a lower bound.

## Fit

| Part | Needs | Has | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search parameters | 21,120 games | 21,608 games (4 h) | Yes, by construction; margin 2.3% |
| A decisive L-01 verdict for one candidate | 77 seeds, 8,470 games, 1.57 h | 196.4 seeds, 21,608 games, 4 h | **Yes**, for 2.55 candidates |
| Confirmation | 96 h | the time product accepts | **No** against 4 h or 24 h |

- **The search.** The default plan fits, but it is a screen: 32 candidates on two seeds in the first round. The
  half-width of an L-01 interval at five players is `1.96 × 0.179 / √s` in rate (0.248 at two seeds and 0.175 at four),
  against a distance of 0.04 from parity to the nearer edge, so no scored line can be `MEETS` or `FAILS` after the early
  rounds. Only a gate with a limit of 0 that fails in every game (G-02, G-03) can discard a candidate on evidence, and
  the plan covers 32 of the 90 or so one-step perturbations. The budget would pay for a decisive L-01 verdict on about two
  candidates, but a halving plan does not spend it that way. The precision a search needs to rank candidates is not
  calculated here.
- **The confirmation.** At 100 seeds it is 96 h. The gap to 24 h is 72 h, or 4 lanes if throughput scaled linearly with
  lanes (not measured). The duration is `0.96 × seeds` h for a short list of one:

  | Confirmation seeds | Hours |
  |---:|---:|
  | 100 | 96.1 |
  | 77 | 74.0 |
  | 30 | 28.8 |
  | 24 | 23.1 |

  A confirmation inside 24 h on one lane allows 24 seeds, which is below the 79 that G-05 needs at five players.

The levers are the confirmation sample (design: the criterion's 100 seeds, or the matrix per seed), the time (product),
the number of lanes and the simulator's rate (more threads than the reference spec, since the rate is CPU-bound). Until
design and product choose, the search stays a screen and the confirmation stays out of reach on one lane.

## Open questions

- The time product accepts for the confirmation.
- Whether the plan keeps the largest halving (32 candidates, a 2.3% margin) or a smaller one with room (16 candidates,
  1.63 h).
- `Rs` under a lighter machine load, and at 8 slots on the later builds: the choice between 5,402 and 6,309 changes
  the budget in games by 17%.
- Whether the criterion's G-07 limit of 2.0 and the baseline's own random-play ratio of 2.20 are compatible.
- Whether the confirmation moves to a sequential rule. That needs criterion version 2, because D-09 forbids optional
  stopping.
- For criterion version 2: base the confirmation sample on the pooled gates instead of L-01, state that G-01 to G-03 are
  sized for zero, and correct the 0.044 in D-09 and D-12 to 0.04.
- Whether screening may play less than the full matrix per seed; the campaign contract plays all of it.
